Why the name

A studio that started with one small photograph of my own son.

Nevin Jonathan is my son — and this studio carries his name because the way I photograph other families is the way I hoped someone would photograph mine: unhurried, honest, and a little in awe of ordinary days.

Every session is shot the same way, whether it's a newborn's first week or a couple's wedding day — quietly, patiently, and with an eye for the moments that don't repeat themselves.

— Founder, Nevin Jonathan Photography

Good to know

Terms & conditions.

The essentials, in plain language. A full agreement is signed before every booking.

Booking & deposit

A 30% deposit is required to hold your date and is non-refundable. The remaining balance is due 48 hours before your session or, for weddings, 14 days before the event.

Rescheduling

One-time sessions may be rescheduled once with at least 72 hours' notice. Seasonal and Annual plan members may reschedule per the allowances listed in their plan.

Cancellations

Cancellations made more than 14 days before the session receive a 50% deposit refund. Cancellations within 14 days forfeit the deposit in full.

Image delivery

Portrait sessions deliver in 2–3 weeks; weddings in 6–8 weeks. Rush delivery is available for an additional fee on most session types.

Usage rights

Clients receive full personal-use rights to their delivered images. Commercial use, resale, or use in advertising requires a separate written license.

Portfolio use

We may feature select images from your session in our portfolio, website or social media. Let us know at booking if you'd prefer to opt out.

Weather & outdoor sessions

Outdoor sessions affected by unsafe weather will be rescheduled at no charge to the next available date, at the client's convenience.

Liability

In the rare event of equipment failure or an unrecoverable file, our liability is limited to a full refund or a complimentary reshoot, at the client's choice.
